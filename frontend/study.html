Policy decision-support study

Study of an AI-Assisted Policy Decision-Support Tool

You will use the tool to explore how two policies may unfold under different conditions and share your assessment of the experience.

Decision support, not decision replacementThe tool is intended to complement policy professionals' judgment, not make or recommend policy decisions.
Conditional and exploratory resultsThe pathways describe what may occur under stated conditions; they are not definitive forecasts.
