How Policy is Interpreted

Theory of Change (ToC)

A Theory of Change traces how policy resources and implementation activities may lead to immediate results, near-term outcomes, and longer-term impacts.

01

Inputs

Resources, rules, funding, and institutional capacity available at the outset.

02

Activities

Actions taken by implementing organizations and affected stakeholders.

03

Outputs

Immediate products, services, coverage, or delivery results.

04

Outcomes

Near-term changes experienced by the policy’s target population.

05

Impact

Longer-term social, economic, institutional, or system-level effects.

How to interpret the chain

Read from left to right and examine what must hold for one phase to lead to the next. The links describe plausible causal mechanisms and conditions, not proof that an outcome will occur.
